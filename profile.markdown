### **my motto**

We win when my **work** brings **value** to you.

### **my values**

**People**, **technology**, **learning**, and **leadership** — the principles I use to make decisions.

- Why does this matter to us? _How do we identify and solve the real business need, and not just a technical one?_
- What happens if you're not available? _How do we share knowledge across the team, and not just build dependencies?_
- Do we have the right people at the table? _Have we identified and involved the right domain experts to guide the solution?_
- Will this actually fix the issue long-term? _Are we addressing the root cause, or just the symptom?_
- How does this affect the rest of the team or other priorities? _Are there blind spots or values in conflict?_
